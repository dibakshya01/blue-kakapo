Responsible use

blue-kakapo is a defensive tool. Containment actions are Guardian-gated and human-approved by design — keep them that way. It assists triage; it does not replace human judgment on consequential decisions, and its compliance features are aids, not legal advice. Deploy it only against systems you are authorized to defend, and read what it can & cannot do before relying on it.
