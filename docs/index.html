Why it exists

SOCs are drowning — and the fixes are closed.

◴

Too many alerts

Teams face thousands of alerts a day; 40–63% are never investigated and ~40%+ are false positives. Analysts burn out; the best leave.

◱

Slow is expensive

Sub-200-day breach containment saves ~$1.9M, and regulators (DORA, NIS2, GDPR, SEC) start the clock at awareness.

◆

The AI fixes are black boxes

Every capable AI-SOC product is closed, cloud-only SaaS on opaque pricing — and the scariest failure is a missed real threat you can't see.

How it works

A deterministic spine. Bounded intelligence. Always auditable.

01 · ingest
Normalize any source to OCSF (webhook, syslog, Wazuh, Elastic, MCP).
02 · triage
L1 enriches deterministically, then a bounded model reasons to a cited verdict.
03 · investigate
INTEL · L2 · FUSION add context, SIEM pivots, and campaign correlation.
04 · gate
Any containment passes the Guardian — allow/deny/modify/ask — human-approved.
05 · record
Every step lands in a replayable, tamper-evident, GDPR-erasable ledger.
What makes it trustworthy

Honest by design

No unaudited accuracy numbers. We ship an evaluation harness that reports the false-negative rate on your own data — the metric others hide.

Safe by construction

Crown-jewel assets are never auto-isolated. Containment is maker-checker approved. Agents obey the Rule of Two. Guardrails map to OWASP LLM/Agentic/AISVS & NIST.

Yours, end to end

Runs on localhost or air-gapped Kubernetes. Any LLM — Anthropic, OpenAI, Azure, or local Ollama — switchable anytime. No telemetry phone-home. Ever.

Standards-anchored

OCSF · STIX/TAXII · Sigma · MITRE ATT&CK & D3FEND · OWASP · NIST SP 800-53. Signed images + SBOM. OIDC · SCIM · multi-tenant.

Stand it up in 60 seconds.

Offline by default — no key, no data leaving your machine. Connect your systems when you're ready.
